# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-03

First public release of Orbital.

- Drag empty space to fling a body, or drag a body to nudge it.
- Fourteen scenes, including a solar system, a binary, a figure-8, a slingshot, moons, and a swarm.
- Mass presets from dust up to a black hole.
- Collisions can merge, bounce, or pass through.
- Trails, a time scale, pause, frame, and a light or dark dock.
